Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about your trip? Find quick answers below.

How can I confirm my tour booking?

You can easily confirm your booking by filling out our reservation form on this page with your selected destination and package details, or by contacting our team directly via WhatsApp.

What is the payment and advance policy?

We require a 30% advance payment to confirm your hotel bookings and transport arrangements. The remaining balance can be paid upon arrival at the start of your tour.

Can I customize my tour package?

Yes, absolutely! We offer fully customizable tour plans. You can mention your special requirements in the booking form or chat with our experts on WhatsApp to tailor the itinerary to your needs.

What is the cancellation and refund policy?

Cancellations made at least 7 days prior to the trip departure date are eligible for a full refund of the advance payment. Late cancellations may incur a nominal processing fee.
